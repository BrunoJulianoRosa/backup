Part 1. SEO audit

The three problems costing leads right now

One brand, four websites.

www has the best copy and the current blog but no cart. store sells but has the worst copy. shop is empty yet indexed with the strongest title of all. regelpharmalab.com sells the Immune Pack separately. Every backlink and every brand search is split four ways. A customer who searches the name gets a lottery.

Placeholder and template text on pages that sell.

"(Include details on each of the ingredients as an active link)" is live on every product. "Vitmain C" and "Vitmain D" on the Immune Pack. The store's About Us is Shopify theme text claiming "several decades of successful operation" for a company founded in 2022. For a $90 a month health product, this is where trust breaks.

Test and affiliate products indexed as the public catalog.

"Jessica Performance Pack", "Jessica Performance Pack 2", "Jessica Sleep Pack", "Daavon Men" and "Daavon Women" ($150 a month) and "Forever Fit" all sit in the catalog with the same description as the Sports Pack. 14 products, 5 real. Duplicate content, and a shopper wondering who Jessica is.

The surprise of this round

The store never says who makes the product. "Designed by a compounding pharmacist", "Trusted by 9 NBA teams" and "NSF Certified for Sport" live on the Wix site, on Facebook and on LinkedIn. On the Shopify store, where the purchase happens, the vendor field says "InPack Nutrients" and the product type says "Vitamins & Supplements". That is the whole story a first-time visitor gets. Persona, Care/of and HUM cannot say "a pharmacist in Germantown builds this by hand". InPack can, and doesn't.

A regulatory risk that is also an SEO risk

"Help you control your diabetes", "lower A1C levels", "decreased likelihood of bacterial and viral diseases such as Influenza and COVID-19". These are disease claims on a dietary supplement. Google treats health pages as "your money or your life" content, and pages with cure language and no FDA disclaimer lose to pages that use structure/function language ("supports healthy glucose metabolism*"). No page we read carries the standard disclaimer.
